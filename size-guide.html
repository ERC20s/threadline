How to measure

  1. Chest or bust: around the fullest part, tape level under the arms, arms down.
  2. Waist: around the narrowest part of the torso, one finger under the tape.
  3. Hips: around the fullest part, roughly 8″ below the waist.
  4. Sleeve: from the centre back of the neck, over the shoulder, to the wrist.
  5. Front length (dresses): from the highest point of the shoulder straight down to the hem.
  6. Head (the cap): around the forehead, just above the ears.
  7. Compare against a garment you already own and like — flat measurements are the fastest check.
