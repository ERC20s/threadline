Made to last, made to move

Threadline is a small clothing brand building simple, durable pieces for everyday life. We keep our runs small, our fabrics honest, and our process transparent.

Our story

Threadline started as a two-person workshop with one sewing machine and a stubborn belief that clothes should be made well and made to last. Every piece we release is designed, sampled, and finished in small batches so we can pay attention to the details that matter: seams that hold, fabric that breathes, and fits that work for real bodies.

We are not chasing trends. We are building a small, steady wardrobe of essentials — the kind of clothing you reach for first.

What we stand for

Honest materials

We choose natural and recycled fibers wherever we can, and we say so plainly on every product page.

Small batches

Producing less means less waste, tighter quality control, and fewer items sitting unsold in a warehouse.

Built to repair

Our garments are constructed to be mended, not replaced. We would rather you keep one piece for years.
