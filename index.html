About Threadline

Threadline Clothing Co. is a small brand run by a handful of people. We design a short list of garments, make them in runs of a few hundred, and keep making them until the fabric stops being available.

Everything is cut and sewn by two family-run workshops we visit each season. We publish what a garment costs us to make, we repair what we sell, and we would rather reprint a good tee than launch a new one every month.

Read the size guide before you order — our fits run relaxed, and the chart lists the flat measurements of every piece.

  • Short runsOnly what we can sell through, so nothing ends up in a landfill.
  • Named makersTwo workshops, both visited, both paid on 14-day terms.
  • Repairs, not replacementsFree repairs on outerwear for five years.
